MAST Zernio social-performance scheduler update

Purpose:
- Adds the monthly AIMS social-performance/Zernio analysis report to MAST.
- Keeps it analysis-only and protected by AIMS_API_KEY.
- Schedules it after the existing monthly audit reports.

Changed files:
- .env.example
- src/jobs.js
- test/scheduler.test.js
- README.md
- CHANGES.md

New job:
- id: social-performance-audit
- group: audits
- method: POST
- schedule: 1st of each month at 05:00 UTC
- hook env override: HOOK_AUDIT_SOCIAL_PERFORMANCE
- fallback URL: https://app.jonathan-harris.online/audits/social-performance/run
- target path: /audits/social-performance/run
- auth: Authorization: Bearer <AIMS_API_KEY>

Validation run:
- npm run check
