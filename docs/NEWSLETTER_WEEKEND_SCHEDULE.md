# AI Edge weekend delivery

Friday's AIMS AM operation prepares both editions after the daily issue. Saturday compares the week's evidence and competing interpretations; Sunday reflects philosophically with Jonathan's dry Gen X sarcasm. All generation, review and artwork happen on Friday. MAST only sends the stored emails at the weekend.

| Edition | UK delivery time | MAST catch-up | AIMS hard end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday debate | Saturday 17:10 | 30 minutes | Saturday 17:55 |
| Sunday reflection | Sunday 05:10 | 30 minutes | Sunday 05:55 |

Times are Europe/London and follow British Summer Time automatically. Sunday is deliberately early because the council programme fills the rest of the day. No extra weekend content-generation operation or GitHub cron is added.

Neither delivery slot coincides with those configured starts or scan durations. The 180-minute DAST deployment-evidence wait is not a scan job.

MAST's cache purge (02:00), monthly mailbox cleanup (03:00), monthly housekeeping (04:00), Saturday quarantine review (08:00), monthly Saturday audits (09:15 or 13:30), daily HIVE checks (06:00–06:20), and monthly governance starts (07:00/07:16) also have separate scheduled slots. GitHub can delay jobs beyond their nominal allocations. Immediately before sending, AIMS checks running CI, council and security-scan workflows across all eight repositories. A busy run or unverifiable response holds delivery for a bounded retry, with a fresh clearance required before send. Busy/unknown results are cached for 10 minutes to bound GitHub API reads. This guards delayed or ad-hoc maintenance as well as the fixed timetable.

## Delivery and recovery

The two jobs require AIMS readiness, use zero HTTP POST retries, and share the existing durable MAST run-key protection. AIMS separately claims a durable per-edition/date delivery lease and retains it on an ambiguous send; individual subscriber receipts prevent duplicate emails. A prepared issue is required and content is never generated by a weekend send. A missing issue, failed QA, unavailable sender/audience or expired send window reports a failure for operational recovery.

Preparation may be retried on Friday through `/newsletter/weekend/prepare`. Normal Friday retry reuses stored schedules and existing approved checkpoints. Never force a replay of an uncertain send: reconcile the SMTP recipient receipt first. The hard end prevents catch-up from slipping into the next maintenance allocation. If maintenance allocations change, update the two MAST times, AIMS `WEEKEND_TIMES`/cutoffs, and their tests together.
